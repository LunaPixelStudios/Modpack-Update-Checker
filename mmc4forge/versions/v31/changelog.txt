- Rebalanced many other parts of the skill tree, including evasion and starting XP costs
- The Mirroring Enchantment now works on Gorgons from Ice and Fire
- Siren origins will no longer be affected by Sirens from Ice and Fire
- Pixie origins will no longer be targeted by Pixies from Ice and Fire
- Nerfed the Echoing Strikes spell
- Disabled the Keres
- Significantly reduced the CPU impact of Apoli on servers
- Significantly reduced the CPU impact of Survive on servers
- Capped the number of itemstacks that can be dropped per entity
- Pixie no longer have any armor restriction
- Changed recipes for backpacks, modified inventory size (netherite tier has 99 slots!), added 4 new backpack types (Traveler's backpack)
- Added the Black Citadel (Dungeons Enhanced)
- Farmer's Delight crops will now generate in village farms (Farmer's Delight)
