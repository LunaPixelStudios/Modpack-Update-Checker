### Config Changes
 - Renamed Cold/Fire Damage to "Cold/Fire Damage On Hit"
 - Added base levels for several bosses
 - Added the Corpse Warlock, Sunleia, Scylla, Kamath Dead King, Frostmaw, and Tongbi the Sculptor to the bosses quests
 - Nerfed the "Arcane Barrier" Skill Tree capstone by ~50%
 - Small tweaks to bonus rare loot, springed, stamina affixes (cHin4916)
 - Sunleia now no longer drops a Blessed Amulet (cHin4916)
 - Blessed Amulets are now crafted in a starlit factory with ignitium ingots, a sculk transmitter, esoteric rarity scrap, and 2 divine blood vials (cHin4916)
 - Added Eidolon rituals for Ancient City maps, and summoning a Shattered (cHin4916)
 - Added enchanting infusion recipe for duplicating echo shards (cHin4916)
 - Added enchanting infusion recipe for converting XP bottles into Aureal bottles (cHin4916)
 - Esoteric Reforging tables are now crafted in the Eidolon worktable, and now require an Esoteric gem, Obsidian heart, Eternal Stella, and Eternal Crystal - and no longer require Esoteric scrap (cHin4916)
 - Dragonforge cores now require a Tier 5 Hephaestus Forge to craft (cHin4916)


### Updates
 - Huge improvements to CPU usage, thanks to removing Apugli and switching from the unofficial Forge port of Origins to the official version!
 - Revenants can now command their summons, equip them with armor, and received many other QoL improvements
 - Fae have received a rework adding many nature-themed abilities and wings
 - Pixies now have more base FP, regenerate it twice as fast after defeating the ender dragon, and can consume Glow Berries/Dragon Breath to restore FP
 - Valkyrie's Intervention heal and Banshee's Sonic Shriek now scale with Iron's spell power


### Fixes
 - Mismatched excalibur textures for Alshanex's "Familiar Ring" (Thanks cHin4916!)
 - Add missing translations for high level potion effects (cHin4916)
 - Missing translations for modpack added ability affix tooltips
 - Outdated GUI texture for Wizards Reborn Alchemy Machine
 - Cataclysm Bosses being able to destroy graves
 - Siren Song not interrupting mobs that were already targeting the player
