### Config Changes

 - Lowered number of JEI ingredient rows to 20
 - Small tweaks to questbook design
 - More structures are now regeneratable by default
 - All small mobs can now fit inside cages, including any baby mob, and mobs released from cages will never despawn
 - All very small mobs can now fit inside jars
 - Merged Aether curios into the main Curios menu. **Remove your Aether accessories before updating!**


### Updates

 - Added initial release of **Automatic Structure Regeneration** - The first time you visit most dungeons, they will be regenerated (unless other players are currently inside). Does not work if the structure contains any claimed chunks
 - Added the **Wayfinder's Medallion**, an item which is NOT consumable (unlike Challenge Orbs), and can be used to trigger your free one-time regeneration of a dungeon, even if it has been recently looted.
