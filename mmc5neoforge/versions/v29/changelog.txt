 - 🔵 Updated 50 mods


### Config Changes

 - Lowered Accessory slot amounts: Now 1 shoes, 1 wrist, 1 hands, 1 charm.
 - Temporarily disable the Cleanse cast affix due to a potential rendering crash
 - Added 5 selections for Starter Kits
 - Spell damage (from Iron's+addons and Ars Nouveau) is now treated as magic damage
 - Disabled Friends and Foes' copper golem
 - Disabled Ice and Fire's base copper gear


### Updates

 - Added Excalibur textures for Mowzie's Mobs (thanks Ruseki!)
 - Added more fitting combat animations for weapons from Iron's Spellbooks and Hazen n' Stuff (thanks RikkaDev!)
 - 11 new structures! Additional buildings and base houses (Farmers Structures)
 - Added Crows, Scarecrows, Nests, Hay Rugs, Corn Stalks, Corn Mazes, Moonshine, Mums (Hearth and Harvest)


### Fixes

 - Level-up items dropping from blocks, and being too common
 - Rings/Necklaces having no gems that could be socketed into them
 - Inventory slot desyncing in multiplayer when players are hit by a mob (Fixes attacks randomly not working)
