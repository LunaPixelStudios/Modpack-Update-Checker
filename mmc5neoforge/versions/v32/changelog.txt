### Config Changes
 - Blacklisted Fast Item Frames from Carryon
 - Regenerated the relics config due to new changes (any previous nerfs have been undone for now)
 - Added fake date config to block seasonal events from a few problematic mods


### Updates
 - Adjusted windup time and animation for vanilla Mace, to be more instant-like (Better Combat)
 - The following backpack upgrades can now be used when the backpack is placed as a block: Magnet, Pickup, Feeding, Refill, Furnace, Smoker, and Blast Furnace (Traveler's Backpack)
 - Added Field of Mourning (Replaced Finality of Decay), Summons graves which each summon a horde of zombies and skeletons each (ESS: Requiem)
 - Added falling leaves to all Biomes We've Gone trees (Vanilla Backport)


### Fixes
 - Fixed being unable to attack after dying during the windup phase of an attack (Better Combat)
