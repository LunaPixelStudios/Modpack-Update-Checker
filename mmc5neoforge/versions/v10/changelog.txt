## MMC5 v10
**[Magic Update]**

**Mods:**

- Added [GTBC's Spellbooks - Iron's Spells Addon (by GameTechBC)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/gtbcs-spellbooks)
- Added [Discerning The Eldritch (by Ace_The_Eldritch_King)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/discerning-the-eldritch)

**Changes:**

- Fixed Iron's spells being far weaker than intended
- Updated the Crash Assistant screen for better clarity
- Improved compatibility with Entity Interaction Range attribute (Better Combat)
- Touched up the Dark Forest biome with new Dark Oak variants, reduced giant mushrooms, and darker atmosphere (Geophilic)
- Added Hunter Familiar ability to track item-dropping entities (Alshanex Familiars)
- Added Mage Familiar ability to grant charged effect periodically (Alshanex Familiars)
- Added Archmage Familiar ability to transform spell scrolls (Alshanex Familiars)
- Added Druid Familiar ability to bonemeal nearby crops (Alshanex Familiars)
- Added Necromancer Familiar ability to make nearby undead fight each other (Alshanex Familiars)
- Added Summoner Familiar ability to deactivate nearby sculk shriekers (Alshanex Familiars)
- Added Exalted Aurum armor (Armor of the Ages)
- Reworked Spore Sack abilities (Relics)
- Slightly reworked relic generation in chests (Relics)
- Slightly adjusted the balance of Shadow Glaive (Relics)

**Fixes:**

- Water generating on top of trees in Stony Shores (Geophilic)
- Iron's spells being far weaker than intended
- Hunter familiar AI attacking animals (Alshanex Familiars)
- Shadow Glaive targeting dead mobs (Relics)
- Safe recipe (Supplementaries)
- Backpack not retaining its name when placed on the ground (Traveler's Backpack)
- Backpack being equipped in the wrong slot on entities (Traveler's Backpack)
- Bug that caused items/upgrades to disappear if inventory didn't initialize properly (Traveler's Backpack)
