### Updates

 - Added Excalibur Texture support for Eternal Nether (thanks Ruseki!)
 - Added Wizard armorset. Has interchangeable hood/hat variants for the helmet.
 - All Arcane Cloth armors are now crafted from the Wizard set using a smithing table
 - Reworked the summon system, summon spells now use the recast bar, which will unsummon the mob. Buffed most summon spells to compensate.
 - Revamped the mob stealth system! Armor makes sound when walking, armor pieces have weights that change their volume, and mobs have different levels of vision/sight based on type.
