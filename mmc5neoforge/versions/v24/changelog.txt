# **[Bosses Update]**

## Mod Changes

 - 🟢 Added [Bosses'Rise ](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/bossesrise) (by blockfactorystudio)
 - 🟢 Added [Disconnect Packet Fix](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/disconnect-packet-fix) (by ascpixi)
 - 🟢 Added [Pufferfish's Biome Dither [Fabric & Forge & NeoForge]](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/puffish-biome-dither) (by Pufferfish)
 - 🔴 Removed [TerraBlenderFix](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/terrablenderfix) (Many incompatibilities)
 - 🔵 Updated 71 mods


### Config Changes

 - Lowered the max level of Backstabbing (8 -> 4)
 - Lowered the max level of all Protection enchants (8-11 -> 5)
 - Renamed Ars Nouveau Spell Power -> Source Potency
 - Disabled Spell Power "unification" between Ars and Irons
 - Greatly reduced the amount of armor high level bosses have
 - Added mob levels and quests for Bosses'Rise
 - Blacklisted villagers, traders, and most bosses from dropping spawn eggs with Capturing
 - Lowered the spawn weight of Mutant mobs (0.05 -> 0.01)
 - Warlord gems are now unique
 - Lowered crit chance on Samurai gems (50% -> 25%)
 - Lowered crit damage on Warlord gems (70% -> 50%)
 - Lowered crit chance from affixing (55% -> 25%)
 - Increased crit damage from affixing (40% -> 50%)
 - Vein mining now works on any block, not just ores (still requires the Vein Mining enchantment)
 - Several nerfs/adjustments to Reliquified Twilight Forest's Lich Crown gems
 - Buffed Dragongsteel weapons (9 base damage -> 13 base damage)


### Updates

 - Familiars Rework -  completely overhauled the summoning system, added a new Frostling familiar, and much more! (Alshanex's Familiars)
 - Added Excalibur textures for Bosses'Rise and Realm RPG | Fallen Adventurers, updated Excalibur textures for Amendments, Friends&Foes, Illager Invasion, Vanilla Backport (Thanks Ruseki!)
 - Updated Excalibur textures for Apotheosis and Mutant Monsters (Thanks BoFill_Pablito!)
 - Buffed the spell power bonus on many gems
 - Added Spell Trigger affixes (cast spells automatically on-hit/on-heal/on-damage taken)
 - Added Mana Cost reduction affixes (for individual schools - weapons only)
 - Spellbooks can now receive affixes and gem scokets (slightly fewer than staves)
 - Added Peanut Butter Pie and Roasted Peanuts (Hearth and Harvest)


### Fixes

 - Nether Forests not generating properly
 - Grass/Gravel blocks appearing throughout desert biomes
 - Fireblossom being unobtainable
 - A few affixes being assigned to the wrong category
 - Random crash when killing mobs while using the Poaching enchantment
 - All generic Spell Power bonuses being far weaker than intended
