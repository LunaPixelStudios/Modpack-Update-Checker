### Config Changes

 - Added mob levels! For now, dimensions or distance have only a small effect, but entities have innate levels - Ignis is level 120, cows are generally level 0, etc.
 - Greatly increased the buffs mob receive from higher World Tiers, and added more World Tier player buffs
 - Blacklisted most curios from receiving Apotheosis rarities. Necklaces, rings, and gloves are still reforgable
 - Disabled the Creative Flight and Elytra Flight affixes
 - Bosses will now heal when they kill a player - a % of their max health based on difficulty
 - Lowered the spawn rate of relics in chests from 0.33 to 0.05
 - Lowered the spawn weight of Hunter Belt relics from 100 to 30
 - Lowered the spawn weight of Phantom Boots relics from 100 to 5, made them exclusive to The End
 - Many changes to Complementary shader presets


### Updates

 - Target Dummies can no longer be used to spawn Treasure Goblins
 - Target Dummies can no longer be used to gain Rage Glove charge stacks or spread experience
 - Mobs will now display their level and world tier next to their name
 - Added Cypress Wetlands, Red Rock Peaks biomes. Revamped Pumpkin Valley and Redrock Valley. New villagers and village types (Oh the Biomes We've Gone)
 - Improvements in AI, new visuals, and bugfixes (Hackers n' Slashers)


### Fixes

 - Luna worldsharing not working properly
 - Lag caused by leaf particles when breaking a tree
 - Lag if shaders were enabled in the Aether dimension
