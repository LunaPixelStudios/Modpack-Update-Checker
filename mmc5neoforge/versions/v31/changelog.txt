### Config Changes

 - Logs will now upload to gnomebot.dev instead of mclo.gs
 - Updated the questbook for changes to Bosses'Rise


### Updates

 - Shader clouds will now light up when lightning strikes during a thunder storm (Euphoria Patches)
 - Holding the tool swap key now opens up a radial menu, instead of requiring scrolling (Traveler's Backpack)
 - Cryomancers are now neutral and will trade. All merchant wizards trade runes instead of dropping them on death (Iron's Spellbooks)
 - Overhauled the Arena (Moog's Soaring Structures)
 - Added the Seeker, a new squid-like flying hostile mob (Eternal Starlight)
 - Added the Tentacle Spike, a whip weapon dropped by Seekers (Eternal Starlight)
 - Added the Dragoon Familiar, an ender themed familiar that spawns in the end islands, and more important, the first melee oriented familiar (Alshanex's Familiars)
 - Added leaf litter, firefly bushes, and falling leaves to the Twilgiht Forest (Van Backport Compat)
 - Huge performance and stability updates with a rewrite, now no longer based on MCreator (Bosses'Rise)


### Fixes

 - Mana from Iron's Jewelry applying to the wrong mana pool
 - Spell power/elemental gems being too rare compared to other gems
 - Removed uses of Streams in hot paths, increasing overall performance (Accessories)
 - Levelup Items not being usable on Tamed Wolves, or other mobs that override right click behavior
