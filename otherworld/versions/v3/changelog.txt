## Mod Changes

 - 🟢 Added [Shoulder Surfing Iron's Spells Integration](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/shoulder-surfing-irons-spells-integration) (by Ellet)
 - 🔴 Removed Noisium  (Archived by developer)
 - 🔴 Removed Villagers Refreshed + Fresh Animations (Archived / Texture Issues)
 - 🔴 Removed Golems Refreshed (Not needed)
 - 🔵 Updated 2 mods


### Config Changes

 - Buffed Legendary Survival Overhaul Bandage healing 0.1 -> 0.5 health per tick (same as Valoria bandages)
 - Unbound Soulbound weapon key from R (didn't actually conflict, but was confusing)
 - Iron's Spells Greater Healing Potion and Valoria Vials now restore thirst
 - Changed a few experience rewards near the end of the campaign
 - The "Health and Regeneration" quest now gives 16 bandages and 4 potions, instead of 1 bandage
 - Updated the loading screen background
 - Lowered thirst drain from sprinting, jumping, block breaking, and attacking by ~50%
 - Fixed optional Lifestealer quest in Chapter 1 not completing
 - Hid donation prompt in video settings


### Updates

 - Casting a spell, cantrip or Race/Class ability while in third person mode now turns you toward your crosshair before firing
 - Attacking with a weapon now properly turns you toward your crosshair before swinging
 - Buffed Dragonborn breath abilities
 - Added cooldown bars for Dragonborn Breath, Infernal Legacy, College of Lore
 - Updated cooldown bar textures for Cantrips
 - Wizard, Sorcerer, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Eldritch Knight can now choose a Cantrip
 - High Elves (not just High Half-Elves) can now choose a Cantrip
 - Removed +1 Magic bonus from High Elf/High Half-Elf  to compensate cantrip
 - Nerfed attack damage bonus +3 -> +1 for Eldritch Knight to compensate cantrip


### Fixes

 - Patched a crash triggered by talking to the Aqua GrandMaster
 - Lightning/Magic damage modifiers not working properly
 - Rare crash caused by having shaders enabled while viewing a Fantasy Furniture bed (or clicking the Health and Regeneration quest)
 - Zombie piglins randomly teleporting to y=-65 (Medieval Buildings)
