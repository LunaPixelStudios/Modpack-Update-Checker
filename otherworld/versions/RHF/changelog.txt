## Raven's High Fantasy
- Otherworld will most likely no longer be updated, unless someone else decides to pick it up.
- No worries, you'll still be able to play v8HF2 indefinitely!
- I highly recommend checking out its spiritual successor, Raven's High Fantasy.
- Raven's High Fantasy is not affiliated with Luna Pixel Studios, the official owner of this pack, however, it was made by the same developer (MuonR).
- It's for 1.21.1 Neoforge, so your worlds from this pack won't be compatible.
