# Archery Update
## Mod Changes
 - 🔵 Updated 4 mods


### Updates
 - Add 3 new Archery-focused feats: Momentum, Skybound, Hail of Arrows
 - Reworked the Sharpshooter feat - now increases your arrow speed and accuracy + After charging for 3 seconds, your arrows pierce additional targets
 - The Mobile feat now prevents slowdown while using any item (charging a bow, drinking a potion, etc.)
 - Alchemists now have improved duration on their *crafted* potions (all types) instead of effects they receive
 - Alchemist's Chemical Mastery now also applies an additional amplifier level to crafted potions of the mastery type


### Fixes
 - Properly fixed a ServerHangWatchdog crash/Worldgen deadlock introduced in v6
 - Way of the Open Hand Monk's Quivering Palm being usable at level 1
 - Gloom Stalker's Shadowy Dodge power being hidden
 - Patched a tooltip conflict that caused some Valoria items to have "Tropics" logos in their description
 - Arcane Ward and Druid-Wildshape-death-prevention not working due to Hardcore Revival (knockout)
