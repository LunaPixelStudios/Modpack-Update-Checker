### Config Changes

 - Campaign: Chapter 6 (and the entire campaign) are now complete and fully playable! Changed final reward items for the end of Chapter 2, and many other reward changes. Fixed a critical bug with Chapter 5.
 - Revamped the Tutorial quest chapter, added much more crucial info for beginners
 - Added thirst values for consumables from Aether, Alex's Caves, Alex's Mobs, BetterEnd, BWG, Create, Dungeon's Delight, Ender's Delight, Mineral Delight, Regions Unexplored, Realm RPG, Valoria
 - Disabled the generic Curio slot, and the unused Feet slot
 - Lowered the loot rate of Echoes by 60%
 - Frostward/Fireward/Poisonward rings can no longer be crafted, only found in loot
 - Nerfed Angel Wings, Fortify, Oakskin, Root


### Updates

 - Doubled the mana bonus from Magic stat's Mana Spring passive
 - Added a new capstone to the Magic skill: **Ancient Wisdom** - Increases the level of all spells by 1.
 - Projectiles and other non-living entities should now never have a nametag or health bar
 - Summoned mobs will no longer have a mob level
 - Some improvements to worldgen performance
 - Adding an upgrade orb to an item will allow it to roll school-specific affixes of that type
 - Added and rebalanced a few other affixes
 - Rebalanced a few boss levels

### Fixes

 - Keybind conflict with spell wheel/shaders
 - Crash when viewing other players' corpses
 - Broken textures for Summoned Skeletons
 - Wayfinder's Medallion not being craftable
 - Bugged nametag/health bar positioning for some mobs
 - Log spam related to "create:goggle_info"
