# [Content Update]

This update adds [Starcatcher](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/starcatcher), a wonderfully polished fishing overhaul mod! There are 100+ new fishes ready for you to catch and record in your catalogue, and with integration with Farmer's Delight, you can cook and eat them all!

[Moog's Nether Structures](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mns-moogs-nether-structures) also got a major update, which added the Mega Fortress - A massive new nether fortress made up of 207 pieces and packed with loot, mobs and traps. It's best structure yet across all of Moogs' structure mods!

## Mod Changes

 - 🟢 Added [Starcatcher](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/starcatcher) (by wdiscute)
 - 🟢 Added [Starcatcher's Delight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/starcatchers-delight) (by wdiscute)
 - 🟢 Added [IrisSearch](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/irissearch) (by SpacEagle17)
 - 🔵 Updated 93 mods

### Updates

 - ([Moog's Nether Structures](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mns-moogs-nether-structures)) Added the Mega Fortress - A massive new nether fortress made up of 207 pieces and packed with loot, mobs and traps. The best structure yet across all Moogs structure mods!
 - Added Iris Search! You can now search through shader settings to instantly find what you're looking for!
 - Updated to Complementary Shaders r5.8.1 + Euphoria Patches 1.9.3, adding support for Photonics and many shader improvements!
 - Overhauled biome colors, added integration with Climate Rivers ([Seasonal Integration](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/seasonal-integration))
 - 40 new structures added to ([Farmers Structures](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/farmers-structures))! Including the cat house, flower beds, pond with frogs, blacksmith's workshop with a mine and many others.
