[Content Update]
Mod Changes
 - Added [Mowzies Mobs](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mowzies-mobs) (by bobmowzie)
 - Added [Advanced Loot Info](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/advanced-loot-info) (by yanny7777)
 - Updated 19 mods


Config Changes
 - Added several quests for Mowzie's Mobs
 - Crash when changing certain video settings
 - All small mobs can now fit inside cages, including any baby mob, and mobs released from cages will never despawn
 - All very small mobs can now fit inside jars
 - Disabled the Compass and Recovery Compass accessories, as Xaeros Minimap makes these irrelevant
 - Disabled text background of Accessorify overlays
 - Hid the ClientSort buttons in the Accessories menu

Updates
 - The clickable sort buttons, alongside everything in the Accessories menu, now has full parity with the Mandalas GUI resourcepack ([Mandala's GUI - Dark Mode Mod Compatibility](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/unofficial-mandalas-gui-dark-mode-mod))


Fixes
 - Several issues with graves duplicating items or not being openable, alongside a rare issue where you would be unable to respawn ([You're in Grave Danger](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/youre-in-grave-danger))
 - Buttons staying highlighted after completion of an action ([Client Sort](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/clientsort))
