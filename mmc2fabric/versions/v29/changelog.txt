### Config Changes

 - Huge revamp to the questbook! Designs on every page, new tutorial chapters, and improvements to almost every quest.
 - Moved Aether Accessories into the main Accessories menu - **REMOVE YOUR AETHER ACCESSORIES BEFORE UPDATING**!
 - Increased the XP capacity of Archon experience pouches by ~2x
 - Buffed Dragon Chestplate (10->12 armor), Dragon Leggings (9->10 armor), Dragon Swords (8 -> 10 damage)
 - Decreased the spawn weight of Kobolds, disabled Kobold item stealing
 - Made structures slightly more common overall
 - Adjusted the recipe for Scorchful's Waterskin, now requires a nether star
 - Moved Nature's Compass overlay to prevent conflict wtih Jade
 - All small mobs can now fit inside cages, including any baby mob, and mobs released from cages will never despawn
 - All very small mobs can now fit inside jars


### Fixes

 - Overworld having random patches of `deferred_placeholder` biomes
 - Arrows freezing midair when hitting a player, instead of dealing damage
 - Crash with Supplementaries sliding blocks
