## MMC3 Fabric v13

**[Compatibility Update]**

*Fabric Loader is now 0.16.10*

**Mods:**

- Added [Nature's Delight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/natures-delight)
- Added [Loot Beams: Refork](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/loot-beams-refork)
- Removed Elytra Slot (replaced)

**Modpack Changes:**

- Added support for FTB Teams to Spell Engine
- Added wetness overlay for Scorchful to health bar
- Moved Accessorify text to prevent overlap with Jade
- Updated Magic tutorial quest with information on Spell Scrolls

**Mod Changes:**

- Increase attack range (slightly) on all built-in weapon attribute presets (Better Combat)
- Improved compatibility with Entity Interaction Range attribute (Better Combat)
- Knockback reduction for fast attacks is now stronger (Better Combat)
- Touched up the Dark Forest biome. The trees were changed up, and the dark oak now has a new variant, with more interesting crowns, similar to what you can find in the pale garden! The biome is now also a bit darker and eerier. This also means mobs may spawn more often. (Geophilic)
- Added block hit sound upon swinging at blocks (Better Combat)
- Fix Combat Roll not cancelling attack properly (Better Combat)
- Made spell animations more varied (Invocations)
- Spellblades animations updated - new animation for Sonic Strike (Spellblades)
- Fixed Flickering Flame not attacking from all angles (Spellblades)
- Flickering flame will no longer fail to perform its impacts if it fails to teleport (Spellblades)
- Updated Pegasus to be tamable, ridable, and flyable! (Mobs of Mythology)
- Added Pegasus to Mountain peaks for spawn location (Mobs of Mythology)
- Updated Kobold warrior loot tables to prevent errors (Mobs of Mythology)
