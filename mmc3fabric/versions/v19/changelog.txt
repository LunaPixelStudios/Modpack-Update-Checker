**Changes:**

- Added Forsaken Magus, obtain a Lavos' Horn from the other minibosses to summon (RPG Minibosses)
- Added audio cues to many abilities performed by the minibosses and rebalanced most of the fights (RPG Minibosses)
- Added the Templar: This deceptively fast holy warrior moves with purpose, and wields a mighty warstaff coursing with divine energies. (RPG Minibosses)
- Greatly lowered miniboss spawn rate (RPG Minibosses)
- Added a Palladium Minecart: This cool ride made out of five Palladium Ingots will keep you fireproof while riding in it (Mythic Metals)
- Added Palladium Rails: These are Fireproof Rails which are crafted from Palladium, and work as Powered Rails when lavalogged (Mythic Metals)
- New textures for several tools, raw ores, and arrows (Mythic Metals)
- Mythril Drill upgrades are now applied in a Smithing Table (Mythic Metals)
- Fixed Tidesingers Water Speed bonus applying on all armor (Mythic Metals)
- Fixed Midas Gold Swords and the Orichalcum Hammer having the wrong attack speed (Mythic Metals)
- Added a rare Bumbling Beepartments biome filled with Bumbling Beepartments structure (The Bumblezone)
- Added bush patches (Geophilic)
- Added firefly bush patches to the Swamp, River and the Wooded Badlands (Geophilic)
- Added leaf litter to the Forest, Dark Forest and Wooded Badlands (Geophilic)
- Added patches of wildflowers to the Birch Forest, Old Growth Birch Forest and Meadow (Geophilic)
- Added patches of dry grass to the Desert and Wooded Badlands biomes (Geophilic)
