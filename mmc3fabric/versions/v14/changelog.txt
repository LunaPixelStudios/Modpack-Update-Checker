### MMC3 Fabric v14
**[Quality of Life Update]**

*Warning! Items equipped in the spell book accessories slot will be lost, remove them before updating!*

**Mods**:

- Added [Tide](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tide)
- Added [Distraction Free Recipes (EMI / REI / JEI)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/distraction-free-recipes)
- Added [Archers Expansion (More RPG Classes)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/archers-expansion)
- Added [Village Taverns (RPG Series)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/village-taverns)

**Configs:**

- Updated Crash Assistant config
- Fixed issues with iron/thalassium/cincinnasite ingots
- Unified silver ingots
- Allowed rolling while jumping, jumping while rolling, and rolling between attacks

**Changes:**

- Water Spells now always crit against Fire themed Mobs
- Water Spells deal 30% less damage against Underwater themed Mobs
- Frost spells deal +30% damage against freeze  vulnerable entities
- Frost spells deal -30% damage against freeze immune entities
- Fire spells get +30% critical strike chance against freeze immune entities
- Healing spells no longer work on mechanical creatures (Iron Golem)
- Holy spells now always deal critical strikes against undead creatures
- Judgement spell now deals +50% damage against undead creatures
- Fixed Totems equipped in the accessories slot not doing anything when applying
- Improve Arcane Beam particles
- Reworked Arcane Missile spell, continuously fires missiles for 4 seconds
- Smite cast time increased to 1.25 seconds, range increased to 5, animation updated
- Added powerful aura (visual effect) to Magus and Magus robes
- Added an intro the Magus fight
- Fixed Starforge spells not working
- Starforge no longer has Maelstrom
- Starforge no longer performs novas while casting spells
- Added much more expansive and well decorated **Vault** structures in Paradise Lost
- Added Totems of Levitation to save you from falls and the void
- Added **Levita Minecart Rails** to levitate across gaps for convenient travel in Paradise
- Fix empty spell scrolls generated in loot chests
