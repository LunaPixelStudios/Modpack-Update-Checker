### Config Changes

 - Added 8 new starter kit options
 - Slowed down durability loss and hunger loss by about half
 - Buffed most items from Relics, Jewelry, Arsenal, and Loot n' Explore
 - Disabled many useless or redundant enchantments
 - Added dungeon levels for ATi structures
 - Moved large text for biome/dimension/structure/difficulty away from the center of the screen
 - Complementary/Euphoria Settings: Enabled cave smoke, improved the appearance of water, enabled interactive foliage, enabled end stars, enabled twinkling stars


### Updates

 - Enchantments are now capped at 4 per item (except books)
 - Bosses will now heal when killing a player (10% of their health on normal difficulty, 20% on hard)
 - Spells and attacks will no longer damage your teammates or their pets
 - Added fluttering wings + nature-themed abilities to the Fae origin (Fae players will need to reselect their origin for new effects)
 - Retextured the Starter Kit menu and added the player's Origin name into the Starter Kit title


### Fixes

 - Ranged Damage attribute not working properly
 - Client crash caused by being near another player's Revenant summons
 - Several texture fixes
 - Duplicate/unusable slots from Accessories and Witchery
