### Config Changes

 - Integrated More RPG's Skills into the skill trees!
 - Increased the experience cost for higher levels of combat skill trees
 - Increased the available points for a few newly modified skill tree categories
 - Buffed Dragonsteel Weapons 11 base damage ->14 base damage
 - When dying in a claim, items will now still be placed in a grave instead of dropping
 - Eternal Starlight boss music will now no longer overlap with other music
 - Lowered the warming rate from fireballs, being on fire, or in lava by ~50%


### Updates

 - Improved worldgen performance by ~30% (Tectonic)
 - Major changes to the visuals of the shaderpack, such as better colors, improved reflections, synced clouds between players, and more (Complementary Shaders - Unbound)
 - Added 3D splash effects for entities falling in water/lava (Subtle Effects)
 - Added waterfalls with mist clouds, which come in 3 different sizes (Subtle Effects)
 - Revenant summons will now teleport to the player when set to follow, if they get stuck too far away
 - Added new unique weapon Chomp'olotl (Simply Swords)


### Fixes

 - GUI textures for Travelers Backpack Furnace Upgrade
 - Undead origins being unable to fight the wither
 - Origin temperature resistance powers resetting on death
 - Dwarves not sinking in water
 - Orb of Oblivion not working on skill trees
 - Crash when parrots rode on a player's shoulder
 - Demon Fire/Ebonbreath/Blazenbreath not damaging the primary target, only nearby enemies
 - Gorgons being able to jump while petrified
 - Revenant and Pixie being able to bypass trading restrictions
 - Guards and Iron Golems not being hostile to Revenants
 - Banshee's "Hexed" not working
