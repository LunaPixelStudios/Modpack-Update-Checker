### Config Changes

 - Made hot environments about 50% less punishing
 - Fixed some bosses/minibosses being weaker than intended
 - Challenge Orbs should now work on most boss structures/major dungeons by default, and are now configurable
 - Re-enabled the Pixie origin

### Updates

 - Tweaked the starting conditions for combat music to require being targeted by a mob, instead of just starting combat music near hostile mobs
 - RPG Minibosses are now passive by default, have a shared Reputation system, and are less common overall (ANARCHY)
 - Mirroring and Featherweight are no longer treasure-only enchantments
 - The Mirroring Enchantment now works on Gorgons from Ice and Fire
 - Sirens from Ice and Fire will no longer be able to charm Siren players
 - Pixies from Ice and Fire will no longer steal from Pixie players
 - Added mild heat resistance to Dwarf and Alfiq
 - Animated health/stamina bars are now toggleable in medieval-client config
 - Reworked the **Infested Temple**, introducing trial spawners, vaults and more
 - Added the **Kisegi Sanctuary**, a bigger and more lethal version of the Infested Temple
 - Reworked Amplify Spell - Now grants 10% per level critical damage, and only applies to weapons
 - Add more spell scroll textures (More RPG Classes)

### Fixes

 - Some remaining inventory menus flickering slightly (actually!)
