[Stability Update]
Mod Changes
 - Added [Gnetum](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/gnetum) (by decce)
 - Updated 20 mods

 Config Changes
 - Added coloring to the shaderpacks in the shaderpack screen
 - Changed the order of a few resourcepacks, which may subtlety change some textures
 - Fixed many blocks, especially those from Chipped, being uncraftable due to the developer of Every Compat not maintaining his mod properly
 - Fixed ghast fireballs doing no damage to blocks/players
 - Fixed bug where putting a stack of glass bottles in a dispenser would use all of them instead of using them one at a time

Updates
 - The player head in chat will now reflect skin customization changes, such as the hat layer being removed ([Chat Heads](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/chat-heads))
 - Improved the Book and Quill screen by making the background color white again and readding the previous/next page buttons ([Mandala's GUI - Dark Mode Mod Compatibility](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/unofficial-mandalas-gui-dark-mode-mod))

Fixes
 - Netherite spawn rates being way too high in vanilla biomes ([Netherite Tweaks & Fixes](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/netherite-tweaks))
 - Many memory leaks present in other mods ([AllTheLeaks](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/alltheleaks))
 - Crash that would rarely occur on startup ([Server Browser](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/server-browser))
 - Potential issue where water splashing sound effect would repeatedly play while in water ([Misc Tweaks](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/misc-tweaks))
