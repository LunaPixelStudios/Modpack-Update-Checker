Config Changes
 - Added a quest for crafting & using the Supplementaries quiver
 - Enabled Modernfix Worldgen Allocation for improved world generation speeds
 - Improved coloring of some quest subtitles
 - Fixed trading post quest subtitle saying villagers follow emeralds instead of emerald blocks
 - Fixed Pet Collar quest saying Craft instead of Obtain, as the Pet Collar is not craftable
 - Regenerated ModernFix structure cache for improved world generation speeds
 - CrashAssistant will now warn the player when they make too many modifications to the modpack after a game crash
 - Disabled Subtle Effects waterfalls due to them appearing in places they shouldn’t and causing massive lag
